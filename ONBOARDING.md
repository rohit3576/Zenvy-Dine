# Restaurant Onboarding & User Guide

Welcome to Zenvy Dine! This guide will help you set up and manage your restaurant on the platform.

## 1. Initial Setup

### 1.1 Accessing the Admin Panel
- Navigate to `https://zenvydine.com/login`.
- Log in with your credentials provided during registration.
- Your unique dashboard will be at `https://zenvydine.com/admin/[your-restaurant-slug]`.

### 1.2 Basic Settings
- Go to **Settings** in the sidebar.
- Upload your **Logo**.
- Set your **Address** and **Phone Number** (used for WhatsApp notifications and receipts).
- Configure **Tax %** and **Service Charge %** according to local regulations.

## 2. Menu Management

### 2.1 Categories
- Go to **Menu Management > Categories**.
- Add categories like "Starters", "Main Course", "Drinks".
- Arrange the order in which they appear to customers.

### 2.2 Menu Items
- Go to **Menu Management > Items**.
- Add dishes with:
    - Name and Price.
    - Description (be descriptive to help customers choose).
    - Image (highly recommended for higher conversion).
    - **Veg/Non-Veg** marker.
    - **Bestseller** tag for featured items.
- Toggle **Availability** if an item goes out of stock.

## 3. Table & QR Setup

### 3.1 Adding Tables
- Go to **Table Management**.
- Enter your table numbers (e.g., 1, 2, 3, or A1, A2).
- The system automatically generates a unique QR code for each table.

### 3.2 Printing QR Codes
- Use the **Print** or **Download** buttons on the Table Management page.
- We recommend printing these on high-quality stickers or acrylic stands.
- Customers simply scan the QR to start ordering; no app download required.

## 4. Daily Operations

### 4.1 Managing Live Orders
- Keep the **Live Orders** screen open on a tablet or desktop at the counter.
- New orders appear instantly with a sound notification.
- Update the status as the order progresses: `Pending -> Confirmed -> Preparing -> Ready -> Served`.

### 4.2 Kitchen Display System (KDS)
- Provide a dedicated tablet/screen in the kitchen running the **KDS** view.
- Kitchen staff can see exactly what to cook and "Mark Ready" when done.
- This automatically notifies the counter staff on the Live Orders screen.

### 4.3 Payments
- **Online:** If enabled, customers pay via Razorpay. The status updates to "PAID" automatically.
- **Pay at Counter:** If selected, the order remains "PENDING" payment until you manually mark it as "PAID" at the counter.

## 5. Support
For technical issues, contact your account manager or email `support@zenvydine.com`.
